# Business Context for Reply Drafting (template)

Copy this file to `config/<business_id>_context.md` (e.g.
`config/demo_school_context.md`) and fill it in. The reply drafter reads it
every time it drafts a reply and only knows what's written here. Anything
missing gets flagged as "[NEEDS INPUT]" instead of guessed.

Real context files are git-ignored so business details never get committed.

## What the business is
(2-3 sentences on what the product does and who it's for.)

## Current status
(Anything a prospect or existing customer would reasonably ask about now.)

## Pricing
(Actual tiers, or say explicitly "talk to sales". The agent will not invent numbers.)

## What the agent CAN commit to in a reply
- Acknowledge receipt of a support issue and say someone will follow up
- Offer to schedule a call or demo, without confirming an exact time
- Thank a sender for their interest or feedback

## What the agent must NEVER commit to (always escalate)
- Specific pricing, discounts, or custom contract terms
- Refunds or credits
- SLAs, uptime guarantees, or legal commitments
- Root cause or resolution timeline for an ongoing outage
- Partnership or reseller agreements beyond "someone will follow up"

## Tone / voice
Professional, warm, polite. Address the recipient directly. Never mention
that the reply was drafted by an automated system.
